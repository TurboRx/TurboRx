## 👋 About Me

> *"No formal courses, no shortcuts. Just building, breaking, and digging until it clicks."*

I'm aiming to be one of the best in **AI/ML** and **Cybersecurity**. On the security side I go deep into **offensive security**: **reverse engineering** binaries to understand exactly how software behaves, and **bug hunting** to find what everyone else missed.

Everything I know, I taught myself. That's the foundation I'm stacking real skill on top of.
